What's Up This Season

This season has been a remarkable story of resilience. Due to salary cap and luxury tax issues, the Celtics lost Jrue Holiday, Al Horford, Kristaps Porziņģis, and Luke Kornet over the summer. The Celtics are also under new ownership as Bill Chisholm bought the team from Wyc Grousbeck. During the Eastern Conference semifinals last season, Tatum went down with an Achilles injury. With the "second apron" salary cap and Tatum's injury, it was expected that the Celtics would not have the best season.

However, this season has been a pleasant surprise. Jaylen Brown stepped up as the team's primary star, carrying the offense and leading Boston to be consistently ranked in the top 3 of the Eastern Conference. Payton Pritchard is showing how deep his arsenal of skills runs; primarily thought of as a consistent deep 3-point shooter, he has been demonstrating that he can be dominant in the paint, despite standing at 6'1". Neemias Queta has established himself as a legitimate rim protector and rebounder. He even became the first Celtic to record 19 points and 18 rebounds in a single game since Robert Parish in 1989. New additions such as Luka Garza and rookie Hugo González have provided encouraging depth for the restructured roster. Garza has been a physical presence off the bench, doing a great job rebounding and shooting the ball from different levels. González, meanwhile, has impressed with his defensive instincts, demonstrating a promising future for the 20-year-old. Derrick White is classic Derrick White — always reliable with a strong defensive presence, while also showing he can take over offensively when called upon. As for Jayson Tatum, he has made one of the most compelling comebacks of the season, returning to the court in early March 2026 — nearly ten months after rupturing his Achilles in the 2025 playoffs. Playing on a minutes restriction, he has looked increasingly sharp in his first games back, and his return has only added to the belief that Boston is a genuine contender heading into the playoffs.
